1. Understanding of the Problem Statement Criteria 1 — 25 Pts

Modern decentralized enterprises release hundreds of digital marketing and communication pieces weekly across social channels, blogs, whitepapers, PR releases, and ad campaigns. In fast-paced marketing teams, content is drafted by dozens of content writers, agency contractors, and marketing specialists.

The Core Problem: Organizations lack automated, deterministic mechanisms to govern brand integrity, tone of voice, factual accuracy, legal disclaimers, and visual standards prior to publishing. Currently, reviews are done either via tedious manual proofreading (which misses subtle errors and delays releases by days) or with generic grammar checkers (Grammarly, ChatGPT) that lack deep knowledge of the company’s internal brand guidelines, product nomenclature, pricing structures, or legal disclaimers.

Business Consequences of the Problem:

  • Legal & Regulatory Fines: Outdated pricing, unverified performance guarantees, or missing FTC/statutory disclaimers trigger immediate legal liability.
  • Brand Dilution & Customer Mistrust: Inconsistent voice (casual slang in enterprise PR), misspelled trademarks, and contradictory statements erode customer confidence.
  • Slow Review Cycles & Departmental Bottlenecks: Brand managers spend 60%+ of their time answering recurring questions and reviewing minor copy corrections instead of strategic branding.

2. Users Affected by the Problem

1. Content Creators & Copywriters

Struggle with ambiguous, multi-page PDF guidelines. They face repeated rejection cycles without clear explanations or actionable alternatives.

2. Brand & Governance Managers

Overburdened with reviewing repetitive copy. Lack visibility into systematic guideline violations or audit logs of what changes were applied.

3. Compliance & Legal Reviewers

Risk missing critical non-compliant claims or outdated performance statistics across high volumes of omni-channel campaigns.

3. Proposed Solution: BrandGuard AI Criteria 2 — 25 Pts

BrandGuard AI is an intelligent, full-stack content governance and brand compliance platform. It bridges the gap between static brand style-guides and daily publishing workflows through automated, evidence-linked content analysis paired with human-controlled editorial workflows.

Instead of a generic black-box score, BrandGuard AI evaluates every piece of text, document (PDF/DOCX), or image against active corporate brand guidelines and a ground-truth repository of approved company facts across 8 distinct dimensions:

Category 1–4 Analysis Dimensions
  • Brand Voice: Formality, tonal consistency, jargon, unapproved language.
  • Terminology: Product naming, forbidden phrases, preferred spellings.
  • Factual Accuracy: Ground-truth cross-referencing against verified sources.
  • Promotional Claims: Flags unsubstantiated superlatives and absolute claims.
Category 5–8 Analysis Dimensions
  • Brand Messaging: Core messaging pillars & mandatory legal disclaimers.
  • Visual Compliance: Palette contrast, dimensions, and logo usage guidelines.
  • Accessibility: Readability scoring, sentence complexity, image alt-text.
  • Confidentiality: Detection of internal leak patterns, API keys, roadmap items.
Key Innovation: Unsupported factual claims are classified as Unverified rather than falsely labeled as false. Every finding provides 6 explicit fields: (1) What is wrong, (2) Why it matters, (3) Supporting Guideline/Source citation, (4) Severity (Critical/High/Medium/Low), (5) Confidence score, and (6) 1-click Recommended Correction.

4. Complete Product Workflow

BrandGuard AI follows a strict, auditable lifecycle ensuring human editors retain absolute decision authority:

  1. 1Content Submission: Creator enters text or uploads a document (Markdown, TXT, PDF, DOCX) and optional visual attachments. Creator specifies target channel (Social, Blog, PR, Email, Ad) and audience.
  2. 2Automated Multi-Dimensional Analysis: Backend review engine evaluates the content against active brand rules and verified facts using our AI Provider Adapter (supports deterministic rule evaluation and LLMs).
  3. 3Interactive Split-Screen Correction Workspace: The creator opens an intelligent comparison screen. The left side highlights exact problematic spans; the right side offers verified suggested corrections with direct citations.
  4. 4One-Click Versioned Revisions: Creator accepts single or batch suggestions. The engine automatically creates an immutable new version (v1 → v2) preserving change diffs without ever silently overwriting raw copy.
  5. 5Segregation-of-Duties Review & Sign-Off: Content is submitted to reviewers. System enforces strict segregation: creators cannot approve their own submissions. Reviewers can request changes or grant final sign-off.
  6. 6Continuous Governance Intelligence: Every finding feeds the analytics telemetry, aggregating recurring violations to identify unclear guidelines.

6. Technology Stack & Technical Plan Criteria 3 — 20 Pts

Frontend Architecture
  • Framework: React 18 with Vite for ultra-fast HMR and bundling.
  • Language: TypeScript 5 for strict compile-time type safety.
  • Styling: Tailwind CSS with dark enterprise SaaS design system.
  • Data Visualization: Recharts for compliance score trends and distribution.
  • State & Routing: React Router v6 and custom Context API for Auth & Workspace state.
Backend & Persistence Architecture
  • Server: Node.js with Express.js REST API in TypeScript.
  • ORM & Database: Prisma ORM with SQLite for zero-config local evaluation; seamless 1-line switch to PostgreSQL.
  • Document Parsing: pdf-parse (PDFs), mammoth (DOCX), Multer (multipart).
  • Security & Auth: JWT with HTTP Bearer tokens, bcryptjs password hashing.

Most Technically Challenging Component & Architectural Solution:
The hardest technical challenge is preventing AI hallucinations and ungrounded citations while maintaining near-instant analysis speeds. Our planned architecture addresses this via a Dual-Layer Provider Adapter Pattern:

  • Layer 1 (Deterministic Ground-Truth & Rule Layer): Uses regex word boundaries, negative phrase registries, and exact source-matching against active guidelines. Guarantees 100% deterministic, reproducible evaluation without external API dependency or token latency.
  • Layer 2 (Pluggable LLM Adapter): When configured, passes content through strict JSON Schema prompt engineering with temperature zero, forcing the model to cite specific source keys. Fallback logic automatically catches malformed LLM responses.
